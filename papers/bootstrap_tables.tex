\subsection{Statistical support: bootstrap intervals}
\label{sec:bootstrap}
All headline comparisons carry 400-replicate nonparametric bootstrap
intervals over the held-out units (guides or pairs), with paired
differences computed within the same resample
(Eq.~\eqref{eq:boot}).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Gap 2: guide-grouped AUROC on the canonical fully-seeded split,
bootstrap 95\% intervals (400 reps). The paired difference includes
zero: this corpus cannot separate the models on any single split;
Table~\ref{tab:offtarget} gives the five-split picture.}
\label{tab:boot-offtarget}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\hline
Model & AUROC & 95\% CI \\
\hline
GNN (this work) & 0.600 & [0.547, 0.658] \\
MIT / Hsu 2013 & 0.642 & [0.595, 0.697] \\
paired difference & $-0.043$ & [$-0.115$, $+0.025$] \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
